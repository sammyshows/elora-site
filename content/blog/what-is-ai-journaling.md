## What is AI journaling?

AI journaling is writing or speaking journal entries into an app that uses artificial intelligence to analyze your words, detect emotional patterns and themes, and generate personalized insights. Unlike a paper journal, an AI journal reads back what you wrote, summarizes it, and surfaces patterns you might not notice on your own.

## How does AI journaling work?

1. You record an entry by voice or type it into the app.
2. The app transcribes any voice entries into text automatically.
3. AI analyzes the entry for emotional themes, topics, and sentiment.
4. The app generates a summary and tags the entry with emotional themes.
5. Over weeks and months, the app detects patterns across entries and shows you trends.

## What are the benefits of AI journaling?

- **Voice entry.** Speak naturally instead of typing, which many people find faster and more authentic when processing emotions.
- **Automatic summaries.** Each entry is summarized so you can revisit the meaning without re-reading everything.
- **Emotional pattern detection.** The AI identifies recurring themes, mood shifts, and growth trends across your journal.
- **Searchable history.** You can search your journal by meaning and theme, not just exact keywords.
- **Guided prompts.** When you are unsure what to write, the app suggests prompts based on your own patterns.

## Is AI journaling the same as therapy?

No. AI journaling is a self-reflection tool, not a replacement for a therapist, doctor, or other professional. AI cannot diagnose mental health conditions or provide medical advice. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services.
