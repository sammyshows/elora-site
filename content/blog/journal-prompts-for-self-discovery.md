## What are journal prompts?

Journal prompts are questions or sentence starters designed to help you begin writing when you do not know what to say. They are especially useful for self-discovery because they direct your attention to parts of your life you might not examine on your own.

## Why use prompts for self-discovery?

Self-discovery journaling works because it turns vague feelings into concrete questions you can answer. A prompt like "What am I avoiding?" forces a specific response, while "just write something" often produces nothing. Prompts lower the friction of starting.

## Self-discovery journal prompts

- What is occupying my mind most right now, and why?
- What do I want, and what am I afraid would happen if I admitted it?
- When did I last feel truly proud of myself?
- What pattern keeps repeating in my life that I want to change?
- What do I believe about myself that I would never say out loud?
- What would I do differently this week if I were not afraid of judgment?
- What relationships energize me, and which ones drain me?
- What is one decision I have been postponing, and what is really stopping me?
- What does my ideal ordinary day look like?
- What have I outgrown that I am still holding on to?

## Emotional processing prompts

- What emotion am I feeling right now, and where do I feel it in my body?
- What triggered this feeling, and was my reaction proportional?
- What do I need to hear right now?
- What would I tell a friend who was feeling exactly what I am feeling?
- What is the story I am telling myself about this situation, and is it true?

## Personal growth prompts

- What progress have I made in the last month that I have not acknowledged?
- What skill or habit would most improve my life if I built it now?
- What did a recent mistake teach me?
- What does success mean to me, beyond what others expect?
- What is one thing I can do this week that my future self will thank me for?
