## Can journaling help with productivity?

Yes. Journaling supports productivity by helping you clarify priorities, identify what is blocking you, and reflect on what is actually working — as opposed to what you keep doing out of habit.

## How journaling improves productivity

- **Clarity on priorities.** Writing down what matters forces you to distinguish important from urgent.
- **Identifying blockers.** A recurring frustration that shows up across entries is often a signal that something needs to change.
- **Decision resolution.** Journaling helps you surface the real reasons behind indecision, so you can move forward.
- **End-of-day reflection.** A 5-minute recap of what worked and what did not is one of the highest-ROI productivity habits.

## Productivity journaling techniques

### The end-of-day review
Write three bullet points: what went well today, what did not, and what you would do differently tomorrow. This takes 3 minutes and compounds.

### The decision journal
When facing an important decision, write down what you expect to happen and why, then revisit the entry later. This builds calibration over time.

### The focus journal
At the start of the day, write one thing you want to complete. At the end, write whether you did it and what got in the way.
