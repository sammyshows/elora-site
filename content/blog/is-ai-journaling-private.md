## Is AI journaling private?

AI journaling can be private, but it depends entirely on the app you choose. The four factors that determine privacy are: whether entries are end-to-end encrypted, whether the app sells your data or shows ads, whether your entries are used to train AI models, and whether you can export or delete your data.

## What to check in an AI journaling app's privacy policy

Before using any AI journaling app, verify these points in its privacy policy:

- **End-to-end encryption.** Are entries encrypted so that only you can read them?
- **Data selling and ads.** Does the app sell data or show advertisements?
- **AI training.** Are your entries used to train AI models?
- **Voice recording retention.** Are voice recordings deleted after transcription, or stored?
- **Data export and deletion.** Can you export your data or delete your account?

## Is Elora end-to-end encrypted?

Yes. All journal entries in Elora are encrypted end-to-end. Only you can read your entries; Elora does not have access to your journal content.

## Does Elora sell my data?

No. Elora does not sell user data and does not show advertisements.

## Does Elora train AI on my journal?

No. Elora does not use your entries, summaries, or embeddings to train any external AI models. AI processing is limited to providing the app's features back to you.

## Where is my data stored?

Elora is offline-first. Entries are saved on your device so you can journal without an internet connection, then synced securely when you are online. Voice recordings are processed for transcription and deleted from servers shortly after.

## Who processes the AI?

Elora uses AI providers including Anthropic and OpenAI to generate summaries and insights. These providers do not use your data to train their models when accessed via their APIs.

## How do I delete my Elora data?

Open Elora, go to Settings, and select Delete Account. You can also request data export or deletion by emailing support. Your entries and summaries are permanently removed within up to 30 days.

## The bottom line on AI journaling privacy

AI journaling is private when the app is end-to-end encrypted, does not sell data or show ads, and does not train models on your entries. Elora meets all three of these criteria.
