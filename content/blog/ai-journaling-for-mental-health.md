## Does AI journaling help mental health?

AI journaling can support mental wellness by helping you track mood patterns, identify emotional triggers, and maintain a consistent reflection practice. Research shows that regular journaling is associated with reduced stress, improved mood, and better emotional regulation. AI journaling makes this practice easier to sustain by lowering the effort required and providing feedback that helps you notice patterns.

**Important:** AI journaling is not therapy and not a medical device. It cannot diagnose conditions or provide treatment. If you are in crisis or experiencing severe mental health symptoms, contact a licensed professional or emergency services.

## How AI journaling supports mental wellness

- **Mood tracking.** AI tags each entry with emotional themes, creating a record of your emotional landscape over time. You can see when your mood shifts and what might be connected to those shifts.
- **Trigger identification.** Over time, the AI surfaces recurring emotional patterns, helping you notice what situations or thoughts tend to precede difficult feelings.
- **Self-compassion development.** AI-generated insights often highlight progress and growth, not just problems. Many users report that seeing their progress in writing increases self-compassion.
- **Consistency.** Voice journaling removes the friction of writing, making it easier to journal regularly. Consistency is one of the strongest predictors of journaling's mental health benefits.
- **Private reflection.** End-to-end encrypted journaling creates a space where you can write honestly without worrying about who might read it.

## Features that matter for mental health journaling

When choosing an AI journaling app for mental wellness, these features matter most:

- **End-to-end encryption.** Your most personal thoughts should be encrypted so only you can read them.
- **Voice input.** Speaking can feel more natural than writing when processing difficult emotions.
- **No data selling.** Any app you use for mental health journaling should not sell your data.
- **Prompting, not prescribing.** The AI should ask questions and reflect, not diagnose or give medical advice.
