## How does voice journaling work?

Voice journaling is speaking your journal entry aloud while an app records and transcribes your speech into text. The app then analyzes the transcribed text the same way it analyzes a typed entry, generating summaries, emotional themes, and insights.

## The steps of voice journaling

1. Open the voice journaling feature and tap record.
2. Speak your entry naturally, as if talking to a trusted listener.
3. The app transcribes your speech into text automatically.
4. AI analyzes the transcribed text for emotional themes and topics.
5. You review the transcript and the generated summary.

## Does voice journaling work offline?

Recording works offline in most apps, but automatic transcription and AI analysis usually require an internet connection. In Elora, you can record offline and the entry syncs and transcribes when you are back online.

## Is voice journaling better than writing?

Neither is universally better; they suit different situations. Voice journaling is often faster and feels more natural when processing strong emotions, because you speak at the pace of your thoughts. Writing gives you more time to choose words and is quieter. Elora supports both, and both feed into the same insight engine.

## What are the benefits of voice journaling?

- **Speed.** Speaking is faster than typing for most people.
- **Authenticity.** Voice captures tone and emotion that typing can flatten.
- **Low effort.** You can journal while walking, driving, or lying down.
- **Natural reflection.** Talking through a problem can clarify your thinking.

## How accurate is voice transcription?

Modern transcription is highly accurate for clear speech, but accuracy drops with background noise, accents, or mumbled speech. You can edit the transcript after recording if anything was misheard.
