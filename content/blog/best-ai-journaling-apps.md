## How to choose an AI journaling app

The best AI journaling app for you depends on a few key factors: whether you prefer speaking or typing, how much AI insight you want, your privacy requirements, and your budget. Here is how the leading apps compare.

## AI journaling apps compared

### Elora

Elora is a voice and text AI journaling app for iOS and Android with end-to-end encryption. It transcribes voice entries, analyzes them for emotional patterns, and generates personalized insights. Key features include Explore (chat with your journal), Soul Map (graph visualization of themes), and offline-first storage. Free to download with Elora Premium at $4.99/month for unlimited Explore and advanced insights. Elora does not sell data, show ads, or train AI models on user entries.

### Day One

Day One is a general-purpose journaling app for iOS, Mac, and Android. It supports text entries with photos, location tags, and rich formatting. Day One has added basic AI features but its strength is in design, media attachments, and long-form writing. It does not offer voice transcription, emotional pattern analysis, or an Explore chat. Day One Plus is $5.99/month.

### Rosebud

Rosebud is an AI-powered journaling app for iOS that generates reflection prompts and analyzes entries. It focuses on guided journaling experiences with AI-generated follow-up questions. Rosebud does not offer voice journaling, an Explore-style chat with your history, or a graph visualization of themes. Rosebud is free with limitations, premium from $4.99/month.

### Reflectly

Reflectly is a mood-tracking and journaling app for iOS and Android with AI-generated prompts. It asks about your day and generates questions and mood visualizations. It is simpler than Elora and does not offer voice journaling, pattern detection across entries, or a conversational Explore feature. Reflectly's premium is $9.99/month.

## Which app is right for you?

If you want voice journaling, deep AI insights, and a chat interface that knows your journal history, Elora is the strongest option. If you prefer media-rich long-form entries without AI analysis, Day One is a solid choice. For guided reflection prompts, Rosebud offers a different angle.

All prices are in USD and subject to change. Always verify current pricing in the App Store or Google Play.
